Privacy Policy

In short: Sailmode doesn't collect your personal data. Your Mac's screen and sound, what you type and what you dictate go straight between your iPhone or iPad and your Mac, end-to-end encrypted. They never pass through a server of ours, because there is none. This website sets no cookies and doesn't track you; it only counts visits, without knowing who you are.

I. Privacy Policy for Sailmode

This part covers Sailmode for iPhone and iPad and Sailmode for Mac (together "the applications").

No account, no analytics, no tracking

The applications need no account and contain no analytics, advertising or tracking. We don't receive, store or sell any data about you or about how you use them.

Your screen, sound and input

Your Mac's screen and sound, your keystrokes, clicks and scrolling, and your clipboard travel directly between your iPhone or iPad and your Mac: over your own network, your own Tailscale network or a USB cable. The connection is encrypted end to end with TLS 1.3 and AES-256-GCM, and only your two devices hold the keys. Nothing is recorded. Your iPhone or iPad keeps the last picture of each paired Mac's screen and a picture of the Mac's model on the device, so the app never opens on a blank screen; they stay on the device and are removed with the app.

Pairing through iCloud

If you're signed in to iCloud, your iPhone or iPad and your Mac each save a small record in your own private iCloud database so they can find each other: the device's name, its network addresses, and the keys your devices use to recognize each other. These records are end-to-end encrypted with keys in your iCloud Keychain, so neither Apple nor we can read them. Apple stores them as part of your iCloud account under Apple's privacy policy. A device found this way still needs your approval on the Mac before it can connect.

Dictation

Dictation turns your voice into text on your iPhone or iPad, with Apple's speech models or, if you choose it, the Parakeet model. Your voice never leaves the device; only the text goes to your Mac, over the same encrypted connection. Parakeet is optional and downloaded once from Hugging Face (Hugging Face, Inc., USA) when you choose to download it. Like any server you download from, Hugging Face sees your IP address; no audio or text is sent. See Hugging Face's privacy policy.

Permissions

The applications ask for the permissions they need: the microphone for dictation and the local network to reach your Mac on iPhone and iPad; Screen & System Audio Recording and Accessibility on the Mac, so your iPhone or iPad can show the screen, play its sound, type, click and scroll. They're used only for these features, and you can turn each one off in Settings at any time.

Tailscale

If you use Tailscale to reach your Mac away from home, the connection runs through your own Tailscale network. Where your devices can't reach each other directly, Tailscale may relay the connection through its servers; it stays encrypted end to end and Tailscale can't read it. Tailscale's own privacy policy applies to Tailscale.

Updates for Sailmode for Mac

Sailmode for Mac checks for updates once a day and downloads them from GitHub (GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). GitHub sees your IP address and the version of Sailmode you have, as with any download; nothing else is sent. You can turn this off with Check every day in Sailmode's settings.

App Store

Sailmode for iPhone and iPad is distributed through Apple's App Store, and any purchase is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; we don't receive your payment details. If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in the settings of your iPhone or iPad, Apple shares anonymous crash reports and usage statistics with us. You can change this in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

Your Rights

Since our applications do not collect any personal data, there is no personal data to access, correct, or delete. If you have questions or concerns about your data in relation to our applications, you can contact us at support@licht.camera.
